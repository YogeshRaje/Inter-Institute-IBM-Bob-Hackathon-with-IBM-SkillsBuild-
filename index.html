The Hackathon Mission

Sambhram Institute of Technology, Bengaluru is hosting an Inter-Institute Hackathon under the IBM SkillsBuild programme. The hackathon empowers student engineers to move beyond theory and build practical, end-to-end multi-agent AI applications that address real-world challenges across Karnataka.

Utilising IBM Bob, IBM Granite LLMs, and IBM Cloud, participants will create intelligent systems that analyse complex localized data, generate actionable recommendations, and streamline critical everyday civic workflows.

This live experience gives participating colleges unprecedented visibility with academic, government, and industry stakeholders while demonstrating their technical excellence directly to IBM leadership.

Transparent Rubric

Judging Criteria & Rubric

Every solution is thoroughly evaluated by two independent IBM jury panels across 4 core dimensions (5 points each, totaling 20 maximum points).

5 / 20 pts

Completeness & Feasibility

  • Technical feasibility in real-world deployment conditions.
  • Depth of architectural thought, design, and modular planning.
  • Completeness and stability of the functional prototype / PoC.
  • Clear and effective leverage of IBM Bob & IBM Cloud technology.
5 / 20 pts

Creativity & Innovation

  • Originality and novelty in architecting Agentic AI systems.
  • Unique agent roles, tools, and multi-agent coordination loops.
  • Competitive differentiation from existing commercial solutions.
  • Creative problem framing tailored to Karnataka civic contexts.
5 / 20 pts

Design & Usability

  • Intuitive user interface, visual aesthetics, and ergonomics.
  • Frictionless user journey and minimal learning curve.
  • Smooth onboarding and accessibility for intended end-users.
  • Polished presentation deck and clear demonstration workflow.
5 / 20 pts

Effectiveness & Efficiency

  • Direct relevance to high-priority local and regional problems.
  • Operational efficiency and speed of the multi-agent system.
  • Potential for measurable civic or business impact in the field.
  • Scalability to support higher user concurrency and new use cases.
📊 Total Score: 20 Points Maximum
⚖️ Final Score: Normalized average of all jury marks
👥 Jury Structure: 2 Independent Panels (10 teams each)

Rules of Engagement

Participant Guidelines & Submission

Follow these professional benchmarks to guarantee an ethical, collaborative, and rewarding hackathon experience.

✅

Do's — Best Practices

Mandatory guidelines for success
  • ✅Thorough Problem Study: Understand your chosen domain landscape before writing code.
  • ✅BYOD (Hardware): Bring your own laptops, chargers, adapters, and extension cords.
  • ✅Self-Reliant Internet: Bring your own mobile hotspot/dongle with sufficient data.
  • ✅Pre-Installed Software: Verify IBM Bob, IDEs, dependencies, and account access in advance.
  • ✅IBM Technology Stack: Use IBM Bob, IBM Granite LLM, and IBM Cloud services.
  • ✅Reliable Datasets: Utilize credible, well-sourced public datasets for training/testing.
  • ✅Responsible AI: Uphold data privacy, user safety, and ethical AI standards.
  • ✅Regular Backups: Push frequent Git commits and local backups of code and datasets.
  • ✅Clear Architecture: Document agent workflows, data pipelines, and system topology.
  • ✅Collaborative Roles: Divide tasks smoothly (e.g. Lead Coder, Multi-Agent Designer, Presenter).
  • ✅Working Prototype: Be prepared to demo live features directly to the evaluation jury.
❌

Don'ts — Strictly Prohibited

Grounds for disqualification
  • ❌No Infrastructure Dependency: Do not rely on organizers for laptops or internet bandwidth.
  • ❌Zero Plagiarism: Do not submit copied or pre-built turnkey projects. Plagiarism is disqualifying.
  • ❌No Confidential Data: Never use copyrighted, private, or Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
  • ❌No Unsupported Claims: Do not fabricate benchmark scores or accuracy metrics for your models.
  • ❌No Hardcoded Logic: Do not submit fake, static responses designed to spoof dynamic AI workflows.
  • ❌Never Leak Secrets: Do not commit or share API keys, access tokens, or sensitive credentials.
  • ❌No Peer Interference: Never alter or tamper with another team's project, hardware, or files.
  • ❌Zero Disruptive Conduct: Any disruption of other teams or the jury schedule will result in removal.

📤 TechGig Bobathon Submission Deliverables

4 Core Evaluation Criteria
DELIVERABLE 01

1. Target Problem & Beneficiaries

Clearly articulate a concrete friction point faced by Bengaluru/Karnataka residents, workers, or local administration. Clearly define the target demographic and quantify how the proposed solution solves real-world pain.

DELIVERABLE 02

2. Functional Working Prototype

A verified, running multi-agent software prototype built with IBM Bob, IBM Granite LLM, and IBM Cloud. The prototype must execute dynamic agent-to-agent tool calls and real data logic rather than simulated or hardcoded mockups.

DELIVERABLE 03

3. The Bob Story & Architecture

A compelling walkthrough (presentation or video demo) detailing how IBM Bob was utilized to architect, iterate, and coordinate the agentic solution. Highlight agent delegation, tool calling, and human-in-the-loop validation loops.

DELIVERABLE 04

4. Measurable Community Impact & GitHub Repo

Provide realistic, verifiable metrics on civic outcome (e.g. reduction in wait times, cost savings, accessibility reach) along with a clean public GitHub repository containing comprehensive documentation and setup guides.
